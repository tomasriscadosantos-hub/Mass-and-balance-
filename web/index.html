How the checks work
  • Take-off: ground roll < TORA and 50 ft distance < TODA. With a clearway or stopway, also 50 ft ×1.15 < TODA and ×1.3 < ASDA. Without, 50 ft ×1.25 < TORA/TODA/ASDA.
  • Landing: ground roll < LDA and 50 ft distance < 70 % of LDA.
  • POH corrections: −10 % for each 9 kt of headwind, +10 % for each 2 kt of tailwind (up to 10 kt). Dry grass: +15 % (take-off) or +45 % (landing) of the ground roll.
  • Pressure altitude = elevation + (1013 − QNH) × 27 ft. Density altitude = PA + 120 ft per °C above ISA.
  • Take-off at the alternate uses the take-off mass, as if refuelled there.

Training aid. Check every result against the POH and the aircraft's current weight and balance report before flight.
